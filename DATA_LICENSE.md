`public/geo/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson` and `public/geo/ne_110m_coastline.geojson` are Natural Earth 1:110m vector data, public domain.

## NASA raster — attributed, not re-proven from the file

`public/earth-raster.jpg` is attributed in `public/geo/ATTRIBUTION.md` as NASA Visible Earth / Blue Marble–style equirectangular imagery, public domain. That is the attribution on record. It was not re-proven from the JPEG. This note does not add a stronger claim, and the raster is not under the MIT code license.

Short citations of Harrison, Ellis, Britannica, the Maddison Project, Our World in Data, USNI, the Yale Avalon Project, Wikipedia, and Grokipedia stay citations of those works. This repository does not relicense them. Links leave the atlas; they are not copies of those sites.

## What the numbers are

Stored `unsure` stays the word unsure. Interpolated and held points display as estimated. Figures are classroom estimates, not certified statistics. See the in-app Sources panel.
